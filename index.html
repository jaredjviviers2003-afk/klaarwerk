Questions

Straight answers.

Do I need to write anything?

No. We get everything we need from a 30-minute call and your existing photos or Facebook page. You just check the preview and tell us what to change.

Is the Care Plan required?

No. Your first year of the domain is included in the build. After launch you can take the Care Plan, or host the site yourself and we'll hand over everything.

How do I pay?

EFT into our business account. 50% deposit starts the work, and the other 50% is paid when you approve the final site, before it goes live.

What if I don't like the design?

Two rounds of changes are included. Most clients only need one. Bigger changes after that are quoted upfront before we do them.

Do you work outside Gauteng?

Yes. Everything happens online and on WhatsApp, so we build for businesses anywhere in South Africa.

Will I show up on Google?

We set up your site and Google Business Profile so Google can find and list you. Ranking takes time and depends on competition, so we don't promise a position. Anyone who guarantees "page one" is guessing.
