Our story

Thirty years of workmanship you can see

Saturn Auto Body started in 1995 from humble beginnings and grew into an award-winning panel shop. Since 2004 we've worked from an ultra-modern 3,000 m² workshop in Gezina, Pretoria.

Today we're a registered private company with a highly qualified team, and approved by Audi, Volkswagen and leading insurers.
