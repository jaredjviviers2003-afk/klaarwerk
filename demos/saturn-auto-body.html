What we do

Every repair
under one roof

From a scratched bumper to a bent chassis: our team, in-house paint bank and spray booths handle it all, with quality control at every step.

  1. 01

    Panel beating

    Accident and collision repairs, including high-speed panel beating for a quicker turnaround.

  2. 02

    Precise colour coding and infra-red curing for a smooth, factory-quality finish.

  3. 03

    Bumper repairs
